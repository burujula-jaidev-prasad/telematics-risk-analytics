🧮 Mathematical Scoring, Actuarial Pricing & Risk Probability Engine

Transparent telematics scoring algorithms, component diagnostic tolerances, and risk probability formulations

🎯 1. Driving Safety Score (0 - 100 Pts)
Score = 100 - (Brakes × 3) - (Speeding × 4) - (Night_Hrs × 2)
Mathematical Rationale for Weights:
  • Harsh Braking (-3 pts/event): Sudden decelerations > 0.40g reflect tailgating, panic stops, and late obstacle detection.
  • Speeding (-4 pts/violation): Highest penalty factor because kinetic collision impact energy scales with the square of velocity (E ∝ v²).
  • Night Driving (-2 pts/hr): Driving between 11:00 PM – 05:00 AM carries 3.2× higher mortality due to circadian fatigue and reduced visibility.
💰 2. Dynamic PHYD Premium Formula
Dynamic Prem = Base (₹15,000) + Risk_Adjustment + Alert_Fee
Dynamic Pricing Brackets:
  • Score ≥ 80 (Safe Tier): -₹3,000 Discount → ₹12,000 / yr (You save ₹6,000 vs ₹18k flat).
  • Score 60–79 (Average Tier): ₹0 Adjustment → ₹15,000 / yr (You save ₹3,000 vs ₹18k flat).
  • Score < 60 (Risky Tier): +₹3,000 Risk Surcharge → ₹18,000 / yr.
  • OBD-II Component Alert: +₹1,000 Maintenance Surcharge → ₹19,000 / yr.
📉 3. Actuarial Crash Claim Probability (%)
Claim Prob % = 3.5% + (100 - Score) × 0.55% + Decel_Factor
Probability Escalation Logic:
  • Safe Drivers (Score 80–100): Baseline claim risk of 3.8% – 6.2%.
  • Moderate Drivers (Score 60–79): Controlled claim risk of 10.5% – 18.0%.
  • High-Risk Drivers (Score < 60): Exponential escalation to 38.5% – 48.0% claim likelihood.
📈 4. Annual Renewal Hike Chance (%)
Hike Chance % = 100 / (1 + e^(0.10 × (Score - 55))) + DTC_Weight
Policy Renewal Trajectory:
  • Score ≥ 80: < 5% (Locked Safe Driver Discount & guaranteed zero surcharge).
  • Score 60–79: 25% – 45% (Standard inflation-indexed renewal).
  • Score < 60: 88% – 98% (Severe rate hike and mandatory high-risk loading).
